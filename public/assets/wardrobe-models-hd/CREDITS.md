# Wardrobe Notes photography

Photographs are from Berg & Berg’s [Studio Looks](https://bergbergstore.com/pages/studio-looks) and [High Summer Looks](https://bergbergstore.com/pages/summer-looks-2), and Drake’s [Autumn Transitional Lookbook](https://www.drakes.com/blogs/news/the-autumn-transitional-lookbook), [Summer Tailoring Lookbook](https://www.drakes.com/blogs/news/the-summer-tailoring-lookbook), and [Zach Zono Lookbook Part II](https://www.drakes.com/blogs/news/drakes-zach-zono-lookbook-part-2). `sources.json` lists the image URLs and native pixel dimensions. The full figure cutouts use the existing local Apple Vision mask helper.

The moving collar and tie insert comes from the supplied `test1.mp4`. The voice excerpts come from `theme_about_wardrobe.mp4`. `example2.mp4` remains the motion reference, including the paper stage, mounted photos, full figures and compact type.
