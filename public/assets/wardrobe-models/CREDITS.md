# Wardrobe model sources

All model photographs and tailoring footage are selected from the four user-supplied `test1.mp4` through `test4.mp4` attachments. The source times and crop geometry are in `scripts/prepare-wardrobe-models.py`. The original English voice excerpts are from the supplied `theme_about_wardrobe.mp4`. The paper texture is the existing `capsule-elegant/paper.jpg` asset. The original score is generated by `scripts/wardrobe-models-score.py`.
