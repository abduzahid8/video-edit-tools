Narration: an original English editorial script, generated offline using macOS Daniel. It is synthetic editorial narration, not a recording or imitation of any founder. No quotation is attributed to a founder. Sound design is original deterministic paper Foley. Existing project paper texture: `assets/capsule-elegant/paper.jpg`.

The clothing studies describe the archived photographs and periods shown. The script's advice about identity and personal style is an editorial interpretation.
