## Reference observations

Both supplied clips are 720 × 1280 at 30 fps. They were used as references for layout and editorial pacing.

- `example.mp4` (36.36 s). Opening portraits occupy the lower part of a mostly white frame, with the serif headline floating above. Around 5–9 s, framed fashion photos slide through the canvas. Around 10.5–12.5 s, a dotted collar guide holds through subtle portrait movement and the headline arrives word by word. The last third alternates product panels with a portrait montage.
- Cerca Trova clip (45.58 s). The opening introduces Tony Soprano with a framed still. Presenter shots alternate with garment examples, while short centered Russian captions follow the explanation. The sequence covers shirts, colors and tailoring before the channel invitation appears around 39 s.
- Adaptation. A 30-second sequence opens with overlapping cutout portraits, slides through a contact strip, and cuts to enlarged portraits with delayed Russian garment annotations. The edit uses two archival outfits to illustrate silhouette, color and contrast. An outfit-building prompt and a real AIWardrobe result screen close the video.
