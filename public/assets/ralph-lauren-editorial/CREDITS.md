# Ralph Lauren editorial sources

Independent ALTA fashion editorial. All 14 photo placements use different source images. Exact image URLs, publisher credits, and pixel dimensions are in `sources.json`. The historic milestones come from Ralph Lauren's [heritage timeline](https://www.ralphlauren.com/rl-heritage-timeline-feature), [Polo shirt history](https://www.ralphlauren.com/men-clothing-polo-shirts), [Double RL history](https://www.ralphlauren.com/rlmag/Ralph-Lauren-style-RRL.html), and [Purple Label history](https://www.ralphlauren.com/rlmag/ralph-lauren-touch-of-the-purple.html). Later campaign photos illustrate the aesthetic, and are not labeled as images from the launch years.

Paper texture comes from an existing local project asset. Earlier cuts reuse a local deterministic score; the 28-second cut has an original score synthesized by `scripts/ralph-lauren-v7-audio.py`. English narration uses the local macOS Daniel voice. Image rights belong to their respective owners; this source record does not claim a commercial reuse license or endorsement by Ralph Lauren.

The 32-second cut also uses locally synthesized paper cues from `scripts/ralph-lauren-v6-audio.py`. No external audio was downloaded.

The Russian v6 version uses the same photographs, score, and paper cues. Its narration is generated locally with the macOS Milena voice by `scripts/ralph-lauren-v6-ru-audio.py`.
