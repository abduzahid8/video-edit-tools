# Wardrobe identity assets

All photographic and moving-image material and the original English narration are from the user's supplied `/Users/v.zahid/Downloads/theme_about_wardrobe.mp4`.

Visual crops end at source y=800 to exclude the existing burned-in captions. Person mattes were made locally with the existing Apple Vision helper. No synthetic photography, product UI, stock media or downloaded music was added. The existing project paper texture is `assets/capsule-elegant/paper.jpg`.

`example2.mp4` was used as a motion and layout reference; its Armani photographs, film clips, narration and graphics are not included in the output. Source times and crop information are in `sources.json`.

To reproduce the prepared source assets on this Mac, run `python3 scripts/prepare-wardrobe-identity.py` from the project root.
